# Refund and Cancellation Policy

This policy covers paid plans on fayazlabs.com. It should be read with the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. Try before you buy

The first level of the AI Product Testing course is free, so you can see the lessons, the slides and the audio before you pay. Please use it to decide whether a paid plan is right for you.

## 2. No refunds for digital content

Because paid plans give you immediate access to digital course content, **payments are not refundable** once a billing period has started, including when you stop using the course or change your mind. We do not give partial refunds for unused time.

## 3. What we will refund

We will refund a payment if:

- you were charged twice for the same billing period;
- you were charged after you had cancelled in time, because of an error on our side; or
- a technical fault on our side stopped you from accessing the paid content you paid for, and we could not fix it within a reasonable time.

To ask, email [fayazshaik1722@gmail.com](mailto:fayazshaik1722@gmail.com) from your account email, with the date of the payment and the Razorpay payment reference. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Razorpay and your bank usually take 5 to 10 working days to show them.

## 4. Cancelling your plan

You can cancel at any time from your [account page](/account).

- Cancelling stops the next renewal. It does not refund the current period.
- You keep access to the paid content until the end of the period you have already paid for.
- After that, your account returns to the free plan. Your account and your progress stay.

Cancel before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.
