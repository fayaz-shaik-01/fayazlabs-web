# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Fayaz Labs (fayazlabs.com, run by Shaik Fayaz in India) collects, why, and the choices you have. We try to collect as little as we need.

## 1. What we collect

**Account details.** When you register we store your email address, a display name if you give one, and your password in hashed form (we never store the password itself). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.

**Sign-in and device information.** To keep your account to one device at a time and to protect it, we keep a session record that includes a session identifier, the time you signed in and your recent activity. While a lesson is open, your browser sends a short periodic signal to confirm the session is still active.

**Learning data.** Your progress, notes, bookmarks and the lesson mode or playback speed you choose. Some of this is stored only in your browser (local storage), and some is synced to your account when you are signed in.

**Payment information.** Payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive the payment status, the plan you chose and a payment reference. We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details.

**Messages you send us.** What you write in the contact form or to the AI chat assistant, so we can reply and improve the service.

**Technical and usage data.** Basic information such as pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location from your IP address. We use privacy-friendly analytics for this and ordinary server logs for security and debugging.

## 2. Why we use it

- to create your account, sign you in and keep it secure;
- to give you access to the lessons your plan includes, and to remember your progress;
- to take payments and manage your subscription;
- to answer your messages and the questions you ask the assistant;
- to protect paid content, for example by showing a partly hidden version of your email address as a watermark on lesson pages;
- to understand how the site is used and fix problems.

## 3. The AI assistant

Messages you send to the chat assistant are processed by an AI model provider to produce an answer. Please do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in them.

## 4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the site, and only as much as they need:

- hosting and infrastructure providers;
- Razorpay, for payments;
- Google, if you choose to sign in with Google;
- analytics and AI model providers, as described above.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the site and its users from fraud or abuse.

## 5. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and your browser's local storage to keep you signed in, remember your theme and your lesson progress, and keep the site secure. We do not use them for advertising.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data, except for records we must keep, for example payment and tax records, which we keep for as long as the law requires. Server logs are kept for a limited time.

## 7. Security

We use encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls and rate limiting. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you can ask us to stop sending you messages. You can also clear your browser's local storage at any time. To make a request, email [shaikfayaz0701@gmail.com](mailto:shaikfayaz0701@gmail.com) from the address on your account. We will respond within a reasonable time.

## 9. Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has given us their data, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes we will also tell account holders where that is practical.
